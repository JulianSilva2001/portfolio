Neurocodec Results Comparison

Results on the Cocktail Party dataset. M3ANet is the existing state-of-the-art (SOTA) baseline model.

Order per sample: Mixture, Target, M3ANet, Ours (Neurocodec), Without Mel and GAN losses.

Key Highlights

  1. Full-Bandwidth Reconstruction (NeuroCodec vs. M3ANet): M3ANet is band-limited to 14.7 kHz (sharp cutoff around 7.3 kHz), while NeuroCodec reconstructs at 44.1 kHz and preserves high-frequency cues.
  2. Generative vs. Discriminative Extraction (NeuroCodec vs. M3ANet): M3ANet uses masking and can leak interferer speech; NeuroCodec uses a generative strategy to synthesize the target voice and better suppress background-speaker interference.
  3. Impact of Mel and GAN Losses (NeuroCodec vs. Ablated Model): Mel loss preserves harmonic structure and timbre, while adversarial loss reduces robotic over-smoothing and improves perceptual naturalness. The "Without Mel and GAN losses" rows illustrate this degradation.
